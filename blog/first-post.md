## Why Cat Grass Matters

Cats instinctively seek out grass to aid digestion and clear hairballs. Our fresh, organic wheatgrass not only satisfies that craving but also:

1. Delivers a nutrient boost (vitamins A, C, and folic acid)  
2. Supports gut health naturally  
3. Adds a fun, crunchy snack to your cat’s routine

## Tip of the Week

Rotate your grass pots every **7–10 days** to keep them fresh and tasty. And if your cat goes wild for it, snap a photo and tag us on Instagram [@norisnibbles](https://instagram.com/norisnibbles)!
