Privacy Policy

Effective August 29, 2026

Nori's Nibbles is a small local business operated in Orinda, California. This policy explains what information we collect through our website, why we use it, and the choices available to you.

Information we collect

When you place an order, we collect the information needed to fulfill it, including:

  • Your name, email address, and mobile number.
  • Your delivery address, handoff preference, preferred delivery windows, and any delivery instructions you provide.
  • The products and quantities ordered, prices, payment preference, payment status, and fulfillment status.
  • Your choice about receiving optional special offers or batch announcements.
  • Messages you send to our order email address.

We do not collect or store your bank, card, or Venmo login credentials. Payments made through Venmo are handled by Venmo under its own terms and privacy practices.

We also process limited technical information, such as network and browser information, website interactions, and a security fingerprint derived from network information. We use this information for fraud prevention, rate limiting, troubleshooting, and understanding general website usage.

How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • Accept, confirm, prepare, deliver, and support your order.
  • Contact you about batch progress and final delivery scheduling.
  • Track payment and fulfillment status and maintain business records.
  • Respond to questions, requests, refunds, or quality concerns.
  • Send optional promotions only when you have opted in.
  • Protect the ordering system from spam, fraud, and abuse.
  • Improve the reliability and usefulness of the website.

Service providers and disclosure

We use service providers to operate the website and business. These include Supabase for database and server functions, Resend for order email, Google Workspace and Google Sheets for private operational records, Cloudflare Turnstile for abuse prevention, Google Analytics for general website usage information, GitHub Pages for website hosting, and Venmo when you choose to pay through Venmo.

These providers may process information only as needed to provide their services and according to their own privacy terms. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect customers or the business, or in connection with a transfer of the business. We do not sell or rent customer personal information.

Cookies, analytics, and browser signals

Google Analytics and essential security services may use cookies or similar technologies and may receive technical information about your visit. We do not use advertising pixels or use order-form information for targeted advertising. You can limit cookies through your browser settings, although blocking essential security features may prevent the order form from working.

Because there is not a uniform industry standard for browser “Do Not Track” signals, the website does not currently respond differently to those signals. Third-party analytics and security providers may process technical activity over time according to their own privacy policies.

Retention and security

We retain order and communication records for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill orders, provide support, maintain tax and business records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. Optional marketing preferences are retained until you opt out or ask us to remove them.

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards and restrict access to customer information. No internet or storage system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and requests

You may ask to review, correct, or delete personal information associated with your order by emailing us. We may retain information when reasonably necessary for active orders, accounting, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legal compliance.

You can opt out of optional promotional or batch-announcement emails using the unsubscribe link in those messages or by contacting us. Essential messages about an active order will still be sent.

Children's privacy

The website and ordering service are intended for adults and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our practices or services change. The revised policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Material changes will be communicated when appropriate.

Contact us

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to orders@igorgeyn.com.
