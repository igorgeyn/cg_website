Our Story

Hello, I'm Igor! I'm a lifelong Bay Area dweller—not counting a couple journeys to southern California and Washington, DC. My family has been in Contra Costa County since 2015. Nori's Nibbles is a project to grow the highest quality, healthiest cat grass using all-natural seeds, zero-waste practices, and local sourcing.

The project began, like many personal ventures, out of necessity: my kitties missed their grassy patio back in LA. I rigged a DIY balcony garden and soon discovered that fresh grass made everyone—cats and humans—a lot happier.
