Our Story

Hello, I'm Igor! I'm a lifelong Bay Area dweller—not counting a couple journeys to southern California and Washington, DC. My family has been in Contra Costa County since 2015.

Nori's Nibbles of Saranap is a project to grow the highest quality, healthiest cat grass using all-natural seeds, zero-waste practices, and—best of all—local sourcing.

The project began, like a lot of these things to do, out of personal necessity. My wife and I moved back to the Bay from Los Angeles, where we lived on the ground floor and our kitties had regular access to a grassy patch on the patio. Unfortunately, once back in the Bay, our apartment was no longer on the ground floor—and there went nibble time for the cats. Luckily, we had just enough room in our new apartment to set up a small, DIY growing operation, so I decided to give cat grass a try.

The green stuff grew like gangbusters, the cats were back in their happy place, and my wife and I were able to breathe a little easier. Nori's Nibbles—named after our beloved little black Bombay cat, Nori—is our answer to the question "What's the best we can do by our furry ones short of releasing them into the wild outdoors?"

We hope you find something you like in our all-organic, all-local collection—and that you tell your friends! I try to make all local deliveries by bike, and optimize the ones by car to consume as little gasoline as possible. Thanks!

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I replace the grass?

Replace every 7–10 days for peak freshness.

How does this compare to the stuff in the store?

From what I've been able to gather (very unscientific, literally just my experience shopping for cat grass for my own cats), the grass we grow at Nori's Nibbles is significantly less expensive and, at a minimum, equal in quality. Many pet stores sell a tiny 2–3 inch pot of grass for $8–10. Our fresh, locally grown options start at $6 for a 4×4-inch chunk.

Do you offer a guarantee?

We absolutely do. If you text/email us with a photo of your issue, we will refund or offer you a replacement right away. We know our cat grass stands up to the test, and we stand by that.
