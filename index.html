Our Story

Hi, I'm Igor! I'm a lifelong Bay Area dweller—not counting a couple journeys to southern California and Washington, DC—and have lived in Contra Costa County since 2015. Nori's Nibbles is a project to grow the highest quality, healthiest cat grass using all-natural seeds, zero-waste practices, and—best of all—local sourcing.


NN began, like a lot of these things to do, as a personal project: My wife and I moved back to the Bay from Los Angeles, where we lived on the ground floor and our kitties had regular access to a grassy patch on the patio. Unfortunately, once back in the Bay, our apartment was no longer on the ground floor—and there went nibble time for the cats. Fortunately, we had just enough room on the balcony to set up a small, DIY growing operatoin, so I decided to give cat grass a try.


Fortunately, the green stuff took, the cats were back in their happy place—including their guts!—and my wife and I were able to breathe a little easier. Nori's Nibbles—named after our beloved little black Bombay cat, Nori.(If you click through the photos, you'll likely find her munching on a patch or two.)


We hope you find something you like in our all-organic, all-local collection—and that you tell your friends! I try to make all local deliveries by bike, and optimize the ones by car to consume as little gasoline as possible. Thanks!
