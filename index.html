Our Story

Hello, I'm Igor! I'm a lifelong Bay Area dweller—not counting a couple journeys to southern California and Washington, DC. My family has been in Contra Costa County since 2015. Nori's Nibbles of Saranap is a project to grow the highest quality, healthiest cat grass using all-natural seeds, zero-waste practices, and—best of all—local sourcing.


The project began, like a lot of these things to do, out of personal necessity. My wife and I moved back to the Bay from Los Angeles, where we lived on the ground floor and our kitties had regular access to a grassy patch on the patio. Unfortunately, once back in the Bay, our apartment was no longer on the ground floor—and there went nibble time for the cats. Fortunately, we had just enough room in our new apartment to set up a small, DIY growing operation, so I decided to give cat grass a try.


Fortunately, the green stuff took, the cats were back in their happy place, and my wife and I were able to breathe a little easier. Nori's Nibbles—named after our beloved little black Bombay cat, Nori—is our answer to the question of "What's the best we can do by our furry ones short of releasing them into the wild outdoors?" We hope that it could be your answer, too.


We hope you find something you like in our all-organic, all-local collection—and that you tell your friends! I try to make all local deliveries by bike, and optimize the ones by car to use as little gasoline as possible. Thanks!
